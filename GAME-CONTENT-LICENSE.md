## Original THEMPGUY material

Original project material created by THEMPGUY and not derived from a third-party source remains under the repository's applicable license, unless a more specific file states otherwise.

This includes original procedural generation code, original gameplay systems, original procedural textures, original sound synthesis, original UI, and original game-specific writing that does not reproduce third-party source text.

When redistributing a build that combines both categories, preserve the third-party attribution and applicable licensing information.
